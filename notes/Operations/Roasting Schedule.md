# Roasting Schedule

We roast three days a week: Monday, Wednesday, Friday. Each batch takes about
45 minutes in the [[Probat Roaster]]. Green beans come from [[Supplier - Finca La Esperanza]]
and [[Supplier - Ethiopian Cooperative]]. See [[Quality Control Checklist]] before
bagging.

Batch sizes are capped at 12kg per run. Off-days are reserved for cleaning and
maintenance on the [[Probat Roaster]].
